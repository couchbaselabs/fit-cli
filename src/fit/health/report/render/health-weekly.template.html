Each SDK's nightly FIT runs this week: how many tests are failing, and how that changed since last week.

Failing
Failing night after night at the week's end, or just started to. The arrow is the change since last week's end.
Flaky
Failed this week, but flips between passing and failing. Not in the failing count.
Nights: With results Ran, no results No nightly
How this is counted

Counted in tests (Class.method), not test cases, over the functional runs only. A test counts as failing from its first failing night, and as no longer failing after three clean nights, so one that stops failing late in a week is listed the week after. A test that leaves the failing count without passing (it stopped running, or now flips) is listed as no longer failing, with why. A test name opens its SDK's page, which shows the state as of today, not as of this week.
