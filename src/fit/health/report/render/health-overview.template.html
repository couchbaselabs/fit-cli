FIT Health

Each SDK's nightly FIT runs over the last 30 days, every environment added together, counted in tests (Class.method) rather than test cases. Each SDK's chart has its own scales. Select an SDK's name to open its report, or a night on its chart to open the report at that night.

Failing tests (left scale) Tests run: passed + failing (right scale)

A night with no nightly run has no bar, and the line drops to 0. Where a run left only its CI log, its failures are counted but its passes are unknown, so the line breaks for that night. The two scales are independent: where the line meets a bar means nothing.
